The active-request-filter scheduling plugin is renamed to utilization-filter and extended to cap any combination of utilization metrics: EPP-tracked active requests, and model-server-reported running requests, waiting queue size, and KV cache utilization. Endpoints exceeding any configured cap are dropped from scheduling candidates.
