The coordinator gains an optional async-broker pipeline step giving
llm-d-async an OpenAI compatible door through the gateway: `X-AP-Mode`
selects enqueue (202 plus fetch by id), wait (held connection with
keyspace notification wake-up), or passthrough (live traffic classified
against the AP's Redis quota counters). Requests without the header are
untouched, and pipeline steps can now register auxiliary HTTP routes on
the coordinator server.
