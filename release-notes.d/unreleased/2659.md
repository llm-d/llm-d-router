coordinator: `replace-media-urls` now accepts `audio_url`, `video_url`, and inline `input_audio` alongside `image_url`, and routes audio and video through the same encoder pipeline as images. These are chat-completions part types: the Responses input content union is `input_text` / `input_image` / `input_file`, so a `/v1/responses` request carrying one fails the model server's own validation and no stage collects it. A media part the step cannot resolve -- a URL-based part with no readable URL, or an `input_audio` with no base64 `data` -- is rejected as a client error for every modality, extending to audio and video the rule the image path already applied; skipping it would shift every later part of that modality onto another part's hash. New per-modality step params cap downloads (`max_image_download_size` / `max_audio_download_size` / `max_video_download_size`, overriding `max_download_size`) and replace the built-in MIME allowlist (`allowed_image_content_types` / `allowed_audio_content_types` / `allowed_video_content_types`); audio and video downloads also check the origin's `Content-Type`, accepting an unlabeled one (`application/octet-stream` or no header, what S3, GCS and presigned URLs serve) but refusing a type that is positively wrong, such as an HTML error page; `image_url` stays permissive unless one of its params is set. On the generate path, a `features` map whose `mm_placeholders` or `kwargs_data` names a modality absent from `mm_hashes`, or names a modality with an empty string, is rejected as a client error rather than silently dropping that modality from the prefill and decode bodies.
