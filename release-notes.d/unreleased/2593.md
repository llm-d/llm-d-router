Fix a bug where a request with `min_tokens` set could fail its prefill or encoder leg under Mooncake, P2P/OffloadingConnector, shared storage, or encoder-cache disaggregation. The synthetic single-token request capped `max_tokens` to 1 but kept the client's `min_tokens`, which vLLM rejects when `min_tokens > max_tokens`.
