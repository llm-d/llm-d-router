The Coordinator sends `kv_transfer_params` and `ec_transfer_params` at the top level of `/inference/v1/generate` requests instead of nesting them under `sampling_params.extra_args`. Requires vLLM v0.29.0 or later (vllm-project/vllm#42644). With an older vLLM the requests still succeed, but the engine ignores the top-level transfer params, so decode recomputes the whole prompt instead of pulling the KV cache from prefill.
