The Endpoint Picker can optionally emit the scheduler's per-endpoint scores in the request-path dynamic metadata under the `x-gateway-destination-endpoint-scores` key of the `envoy.lb` namespace, enabled with the new `--emit-endpoint-scores` flag (off by default). Known gap: no scores are emitted under the deprecated `data-parallel-profile-handler`, or under the deprecated `pd-profile-handler` when `primaryPort` is set, because those handlers rebuild the scheduling result without carrying the scores through.
